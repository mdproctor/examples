# Ideal Prose Methodology

## Principle

Every cognitive facet must justify its contribution to rendered output
BEFORE implementation. The sequence is:

1. **Describe** — hand-craft what the ideal rendered output looks like
   when this facet is exercised (the "golden" prose)
2. **Test** — run the classifier on the golden prose to verify it
   scores 4.0+ on quality and register
3. **Implement** — build/modify the backend pipeline to generate
   equivalent output
4. **Iterate** — tweak the pipeline until it matches the golden target

This applies to every new cognitive layer, every extension, every
architectural change.

## Coverage Requirements

The ideal prose corpus must exercise ALL cognitive layers:

- Barrett 4-channels (body, attention, time, action readiness)
- Beliefs (challenged, reinforced, revised)
- Formation memories (situation-triggered)
- CAPS attractors (personality gravitational pull)
- Drive differentiation (which wins when two compete)
- Personality facets (expressed through behavior)
- Attachment style (secure/avoidant/anxious responses)
- Theory of Mind (sensing others' distress, reading intent)
- Trust (building, eroding, betrayal)
- Relational schemas (role-specific responses)
- Self-standards (felt satisfaction when drive is met)
- Priority signatures (how drives blend)
- Persona constraint (public/private switching)

## Channel Selection Principle

Barrett 4-channel descriptions are NOT a checklist. Each
drive×target×situation combination selects the 1-2 channels most
relevant to that moment. The selection is what makes the prose feel
alive rather than mechanical.

## Scoring Targets

All drives must achieve:
- Quality: 4.0+
- Register: 4.0+
- Differentiation: 4.0+

Per-character averages should exceed Era 4 baseline (Q=3.92, R=3.74).

## Baseline Results

Initial 10-event test (entrance-hall only):
  Q=4.03, R=3.97, D=3.85 — beats Era 4 baseline on all dimensions.
