## Observations

1. **All 5 characters use somatic + at least one other channel.** Somatic-only bottleneck is broken.
2. **Attentional narrowing is the strongest discriminator.** Each character's attention is drawn to different targets consistent with their drives — Ant Hill Mob scans for threats, Hooded Claw catalogues exploitable people, Dick Dastardly inventories valuables.
3. **Action tendency appears in 4/5 characters.** The "pull toward doing" produces drive-specific signatures.
4. **Temporal channel is weakest.** Only 3/5 characters express temporal distortion in their first turn. May strengthen in later turns with more varied situations.
5. **Drive discrimination appears strong.** Protection (Ant Hill Mob) reads completely differently from scheming (Hooded Claw) — the attentional and action tendency channels carry the distinction that somatic alone could not.
